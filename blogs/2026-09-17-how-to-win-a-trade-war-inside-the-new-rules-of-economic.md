Trade wars are hard to win but new weapons change that. Soumaya Keynes, Financial Times economics columnist, co-author of How to Win a Trade War: An Optimistic Guide to an Anxious Global Economy, and host of FT’s The Economics Show, joins Kit Conklin to explain why stockpiling, industrial policy, digital services taxes and other tools once dismissed as outdated are back in fashion as countries and companies race to win in an increasingly fragmented global order.
